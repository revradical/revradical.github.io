Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

NAS Calendar Sync is a personal tool used only by its owner. It is not distributed to or used by anyone else.

What it accesses

With the owner's permission, the app reads and writes events in one Google Calendar belonging to the owner, in order to mirror events from the owner's own self-hosted calendar server.

What it does with that data

Calendar data is transferred only between the owner's own server and the owner's own Google account. It is not sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or stored anywhere else.
